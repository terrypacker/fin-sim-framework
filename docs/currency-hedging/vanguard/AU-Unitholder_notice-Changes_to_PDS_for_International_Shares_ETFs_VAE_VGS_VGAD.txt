Important notice
to investors
Important information regarding changes to Vanguard’s product
disclosure statement for Vanguard International Shares ETFs

We’ve made updates to the Vanguard International Shares ETFs product disclosure statement (PDS)
for the following ETFs:
        • Vanguard FTSE Asia ex Japan Shares Index ETF (ASX code: VAE)
        • Vanguard MSCI Index International Shares ETF (ASX code: VGS)
        • Vanguard MSCI Index International Shares (Hedged) ETF (ASX code: VGAD).

Below is a summary of the changes.


‘Who it may suit’ statements
We’ve updated the ‘Who it may suit’ statements:
       • in section 2 for the Vanguard FTSE Asia ex Japan Shares Index ETF;
       • in section 3 for the Vanguard MSCI Index International Shares ETF; and
       • in section 4 for MSCI Index International Shares (Hedged) ETF.

No changes have been made to the investment objectives, strategies or management of these ETFs.


Exposure period for new product
Additionally, we’ve updated the PDS to include information and disclosures about a new ETF,
Vanguard International Shares High Yield ETF. The exposure period commences today and no
applications will be accepted by Vanguard during the exposure period. On 25 March 2026, this ETF is
expected to commence trading on the Australian Securities Exchange.


Where are Vanguard’s updated disclosure documents located?
The updated PDS is available on the Vanguard Australia website www.vanguard.com.au.
