### Gold

The preset's `inflation` leg adds +5 pp to `GOLD`, one-for-one with the US inflation
leg, so gold keeps its base real return through the decade (+2 %/yr at the library
defaults) instead of losing about 3 %/yr to the inflation leg (design 121 §5). It does not
reproduce history, on purpose: much of 1972-80 was the one-off re-pricing after Bretton
Woods, and building it in would make gold the guaranteed winner of every stagflation draw.

| window | preset alone | preset + historical shock | measured |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| 0–96 mo, real × | 1.16 | 5.35 | 5.35 (1972 → 1980) |
| 96–216 mo, real × | 1.21 | 0.39 | 0.39 (1980 → 1990) |

For the upper bound, add this custom shock to `shocks` beside the preset, with the same
`startDate`. Its adjustments are sized against the preset's own gold and inflation legs, so
it is only right in that pairing, and only at the library default bases.
