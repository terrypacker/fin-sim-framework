 1   3 Definitions
 2                 In this Act:
 3                 income year has the same meaning as in the Income Tax
 4                 Assessment Act 1997.
 5                 taxable contributions has the same meaning as in the Income Tax
 6                 Assessment Act 1997.

 7   4 Imposition of tax
 8                 Tax payable under section 293-15 of the Income Tax Assessment
 9                 Act 1997 is imposed.

10   5 Amount of tax
11                 The amount of the tax is 15% of a person’s taxable contributions
12                 for an income year.

13   6 Severability
14                 If, apart from this section, section 4 would impose, in relation to a
15                 person, a tax the imposition of which in relation to the person
16                 would exceed the legislative power of the Commonwealth,
17                 section 4 has effect as if it did not impose that tax in relation to the
18                 person.
