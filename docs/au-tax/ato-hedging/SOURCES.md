**The point design 120 needs.** With a hedging election in effect, gains or losses from a
hedge of a CGT asset "will be treated as a capital gain or capital loss on the same basis
as the capital gain or capital loss on the underlying CGT asset that is being hedged"
(CGT guide 2021 and 2016 pages; ITAA 1997 s230-310(4) item 1, s230-300(3)). VGAD made that
election from 1 July 2024 (`../../currency-hedging/vanguard/`).
